The linear expression 2x minus 3 changes sign at x equal to three halves. On the first interval the absolute value is 3 minus 2x; on the second it is 2x minus 3. Integrating the first part gives nine quarters, and integrating the second gives twenty-five quarters. Their sum is thirty-four quarters, which reduces to seventeen halves. Equivalently, the graph consists of two triangles with base-height products three halves times 3 and five halves times 5. Each triangle contributes half its base-height product.
