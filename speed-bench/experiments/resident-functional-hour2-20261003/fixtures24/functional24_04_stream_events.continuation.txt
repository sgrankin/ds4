The two delta events contribute ab and c, in that order, giving abc. Each heartbeat confirms that the channel is still present but adds no characters. There are two such ignored heartbeat events. Completion occurs at the explicit end event rather than at an empty or nontext event. In particular, the first heartbeat does not turn this into an empty completed response. Keeping the text accumulator, heartbeat count, and completion flag separate makes all three observations visible. Every event is accounted for once, and the final completion marker changes no previously accumulated text.
