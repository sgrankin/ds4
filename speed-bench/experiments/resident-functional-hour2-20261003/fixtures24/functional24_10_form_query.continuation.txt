The encoded percent sequence %2B becomes a literal plus in a+b; it does not become a space after decoding. By contrast, the literal plus between c and d is the form-encoding space marker. The empty field value remains an empty string, and flag without an equals sign also has an empty value under the stated rule. The final three percent-encoded bytes form the UTF-8 check mark. Both item entries survive in their original positions. A dictionary that retained only one value per key would lose one of them. Ordered pairs preserve this distinction along with blank values and decoded Unicode.
