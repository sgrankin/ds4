From the center square, changing both coordinates by exactly two reaches the four corners: [0,0], [0,4], [4,0], and [4,4]. Two of those destinations are occupied, so only [0,4] and [4,0] are legal. The occupied intermediate square [1,3] is deliberately irrelevant under this invented rule. A reader should therefore not import the blocking rules of chess or another familiar board game. All four geometric candidates lie within the five-by-five board. The explanation separates the movement geometry from destination eligibility and retains the stated exception for intermediate occupancy.
