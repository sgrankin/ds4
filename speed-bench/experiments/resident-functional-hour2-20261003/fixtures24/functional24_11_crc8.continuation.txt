Process the bytes 01, 02, and 03 in their listed order. After the first byte the register is 07. XORing the second byte gives 05 before its eight shifts, and the completed second-byte register is 1B. XORing the third byte gives 18, which advances through the specified shifts to 48. The final register is therefore hexadecimal 48, or decimal 72. This convention is MSB-first and does not reflect the bytes or register. There is also no final XOR. A polynomial long-division calculation over binary coefficients produces the same remainder when the message is augmented with eight zero bits.
