# Resident experts: fewer requested reads, mixed quality and latency

Replacing a missed routed expert with one already resident can reduce logical read demand, but the best policy depends on cache history and can change outputs. Early experiments did not justify making the approximation a default. All results here use the original Flash Q2 checkpoint, not the later 1017 checkpoint; timing screens with fewer than five samples per side are historical diagnostics.

## Choosing substitutions

The conservative candidate replaced at most the weakest native miss, bounded by router affinity gap 0.05, original mixture mass 0.12 and rank 12, with prefill exact. On SSD streaming with a requested 64 GiB cache and 512-row prefill, the locked screening and disjoint held-out sets gave mean delta-NLL −0.000202 and −0.005589 nats/token, mean KL 0.004716 and 0.005058, and 6/492 and 10/512 top-1 changes. Held-out worst-prompt delta was +0.031736 and KL p99 0.066368. Disabled controls were byte-identical to baseline core `67696070` with the integrated backend. The screen does not establish generated task accuracy.

A seven-phase ABBA replay averaged 45.079 s candidate versus 41.930 s native; concurrent PDF indexing and a 9.15% startup-prefill difference make causal attribution uncertain. Candidate skipped 381 requested loads, or 3.53 GiB, which is logical demand rather than physical SSD traffic. A separate profile attributed 12.859 ms of 343-token decode to complete no-op planning; it did not establish balanced latency. [Overhead profile](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-overhead-profile-20261002/compact-summary.json).

Broader two-slot policies on 16 independent prompts produced mean delta-NLL +0.014105 and +0.013860 and KL 0.010601 and 0.012651. The broader three-slot policy on a later frozen confirmation split reduced whole-engine logical expert reads 207.277 to 147.995 GB (28.60%); mean delta-NLL was +0.007225, KL 0.012595, with 20 argmax changes in 512 rows and a worst-prompt delta +0.043891. Its confidence interval crossed zero. Four generated coding tasks passed all 34 checks, though generated code differed in three. One quiet ABBA showed 31.4840 versus 29.8724 s for append plus decode; this single workload does not meet today's five-sample comparison standard. [Independent split](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-broad-independent16-20261002/compact-summary.json), [confirmation](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-three-confirmation-completed-20261002-v2/README.md), [coding](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-three-coding-medium-20261002-v1-native/README.md), [timing](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-three-fast64-abba-completed-20261002-v2/README.md).

A fresh mixed 24-prompt confirmation of a stronger anchor saved 23.10% requested expert bytes but exceeded its prespecified screens: delta-NLL +0.012299, KL 0.023915 and 44 of 768 argmax changes. Its interval crossed zero; requested bytes still do not measure actual physical reads. Functional similarity did not establish an advantage over the simpler affinity heuristic in the subsequent 24-case study. [Mixed confirmation](../../../speed-bench/experiments/resident-mixed24-confirmation-20261003/README.md), [functional study](../../../speed-bench/experiments/resident-functional-hour2-20261003/README.md).

## Can background reads restore the original expert?

The idea was to use a resident substitute immediately and stage the native expert for a later request, or restore it before binding if it arrived in time. In a 16-case calibration, a single READY slot consumed only 24 of 251 staged reads; 226 expired and 5,506 of 5,833 attempts were blocked. Exact accounting showed the staged variant used 177.656 GB total logical reads versus 175.586 GB unstaged, 1.18% more. A larger bounded pool admitted more reads but consumed only 9–10%, adding 12.69–12.81% total logical reads. Longer retention increased useful reads to about 16%, yet remained 11.46–11.75% above the unstaged baseline. Late restoration occurred only a handful of times. These are measured negatives for these policies, not proof that every backfill design is impossible. [Single-slot accounting](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-backfill-exact-io-20261002/compact-result.json), [bounded pool](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-backfill-pool-completed-20261002/RESULTS.md), [retention](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-backfill-ready-age-completed-20261002/RESULTS.md).

A later conditional join avoided the worst cost of an unconditional early join; age 8 was timing-neutral versus affinity with 0.295% more logical reads, while age 1 completed 3/4 generated tasks versus 2/4 and cost 1.48% more whole-task time in that screen. These private prototypes remain diagnostic. [Original arrivals](../../../speed-bench/experiments/original-arrival-hour3-20261003/README.md).

## Decision

Retain native routing as the baseline and resident replacement as a measured opt-in. Preserving original router coefficients produced mixed quality changes rather than proving equivalence; practical task outcomes and tails matter alongside mean KL and delta-NLL. A three-turn ledger study completed all nine stages under native and broader routing, but its original flat validator failed on compaction and resident needed more tool rounds, so it does not establish a task speedup. [Native weights](../../../speed-bench/experiments/resident-native-weights-20261003/compact-summary.json), [ledger study](../../../speed-bench/experiments/ds41-ssd/AGENT_LIVE_COMPACTION_V3_RESULT.md), [recipe](../../../recipes/01-resident-routing.md).

## Other measured boundaries

The native routing controls, including positive-gap native mode, reproduced seven phase-end full-vocabulary vectors and final graph state exactly. In the separate three-slot calibration, delta-NLL improved versus two slots while KL and its maximum increased; a joint 343-token trajectory showed much smaller drift and 10.97% fewer total logical reads. Different fixtures and cache histories explain why neither screen substitutes for the other. [Native controls](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/integrated-native-parity-20261002/RESULTS.md), [breadth calibration](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-three-calibration16-20261002/RESULTS.md), [joint screen](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/fixed64-resident-quality-three-completed-20261002/RESULTS.md).

Late original restoration worked mechanically but restored only 2 and 3 of about 3,500 substitutions, adding logical reads. Rechecking queued experts for native residency removed 31 and 34 redundant reads but the pool still requested about 11.7–11.8% more than unstaged. [Late restoration](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-backfill-late-one-slot-20261002/BACKFILL_LATE_V1_RESULT.md), [native-residency recheck](../../../speed-bench/experiments/ds41-ssd/BACKFILL_POOL_NATIVE_RECHECK_RESULT.md).

Cache size also changed native batching. At 80 GiB the resident candidate's 31.342 s turn versus native's 30.860 s was within large native variation. A separate native 64/80 GiB bracket showed 40.900/31.071 s, but crossing a prefill batching threshold confounded this with cache size. Short quality fixtures matched exactly; the long replay showed mean delta-NLL +0.000222631, KL 0.000303374 and two argmax changes. At fixed 64 GiB, tile 64/limit 1024 reproduced the same printed quality metrics and reduced the diagnostic replay turn from 40.7754 to 32.5250 s. The helper did not export candidate logits, so equal printed metrics do not prove byte identity. A longer 26,659-token run showed only 1.044% append/decode reduction and a 3.656% regression on its 8,192-token append; four medium-append coding tasks passed all 34 checks. [80 GiB routing](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-broader-abba80-20261002/compact-summary.json), [native cache timing](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/native-cache64-v80-abba-20261002/compact-summary.json), [short quality](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/native-cache80-quality-completed-20261002-v1/checks.json), [long quality](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/native-cache64-v80-long-replay-quality-20261002/compact-summary.json), [fixed-budget timing](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/native-prefill64-tile64-limit1024-abba-completed-20261002-v1/RESULTS.md), [long context](../../../speed-bench/experiments/native-resident-growth-six-20261003/README.md), [medium coding](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/native-prefill-coding-medium-completed-20261002-v1/RESULTS.md).

The first ledger fixes passed independent checks but both transcripts hit their 275-second budget before the final marker, so neither was a completed harness run. In later unused confirmation, affinity plus WRITE256 passed both executable tasks and saved 23.90% against native brackets, while a separate generated-answer set passed only 1/4 under every policy. [Ledger artifacts](../../../speed-bench/ideas/evidence/resident-coding/), [unused confirmation](../../../speed-bench/experiments/confirmation-hour5-20261003/README.md).

The subsequent eight-arm environment calibration tried five additional policies; none passed its fixed screen, so selection did not advance. A ragged long-append dispatch qualification changed the second half of 64 teacher vectors: mean delta-NLL +0.05274755, KL 0.00518421 and no argmax flips. Those artificial extensions and profiled single-order timings did not support a default change. [Policy calibration](../../../speed-bench/experiments/resident-env-calibration-20261003/), [ragged-tail qualification](../../../speed-bench/experiments/ragged-native-tail-qualification-20261003/compact-summary.json).

The three-substitution integration retained the previous one/two-substitution behavior in focused CPU checks; its larger substitution count alone was not a default-promotion argument. An earlier native-weight calibration showed slightly lower delta-NLL but higher mean and maximum KL than affinity on its 16 cases.
